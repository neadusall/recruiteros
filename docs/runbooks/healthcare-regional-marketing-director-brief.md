## Director of Regional Marketing

**Company:** A multi-state outpatient healthcare clinic network. Currently operating in
New Hampshire and opening its next markets in Missouri and South Carolina. Backed for
growth, roughly 100 to 800 employees, adding locations continuously.

**Based in:** Lawrence, Kansas. A Wichita, Kansas base also works, with one day a week in
Lawrence.

### What this person owns

This is a growth and demand generation leadership seat, not a brand or corporate
communications seat. The Director of Regional Marketing owns patient acquisition across
every state the network operates in, and is accountable for patient volume, cost per
acquired patient, and conversion rate.

They will:

- Own regional marketing strategy across multiple states and every clinic location.
- Build and scale the patient acquisition engine: paid search, SEO, paid social,
  lifecycle and CRM marketing, and local offline demand.
- Run market entry for new states, including Missouri and South Carolina, and launch
  marketing for each de novo clinic opening.
- Lead clinic level and field marketing so each location performs against its own
  targets, not just the national average.
- Develop referral networks with physicians, provider groups and community partners.
- Manage an internal marketing team and the outside agencies.
- Own the marketing budget and report return on it.
- Work directly with clinical leadership and operations, since patient demand and clinic
  capacity are the same problem seen from two sides.
- Build the marketing infrastructure, playbooks and reporting that let a new location
  open and ramp without reinventing anything.

### Where they come from

The best fit is someone doing this inside a multi-location healthcare organization:
outpatient clinic networks, urgent care networks, dental and orthodontic groups, medspa
and aesthetics chains, behavioral health platforms, physical therapy networks, vision and
optometry groups, specialty physician groups, hospital outpatient networks, healthcare
management services organizations, and private equity backed healthcare platforms doing
acquisitions and roll ups.

Also strong: home health and hospice networks, senior living groups, dialysis, diagnostic
labs, imaging centers and rehabilitation networks.

Worth surfacing as well: marketing leaders from multi-location consumer service chains,
franchise systems and veterinary groups. The multi-site growth playbook transfers, and
these people often move into healthcare and do very well.

### Requirements

There are no licences, certifications or degrees required for this role. Judge on scope
and results.

### Why someone takes this

Multi-state ownership, a marketing function they get to build rather than inherit, and a
seat at the table with clinical and operating leadership in a network that is actively
opening new markets.
