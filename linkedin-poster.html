Save inspiration

See a post you like? Paste it here. The rewriter extracts the idea and rebuilds it as your story, in your voice, never a copy.

Original posts

The writer also creates original posts from your brand, voice, and industries, with the creative already attached. One fresh draft a day arrives on its own while hands-off mode is on, alternating with blind spotlights of your open jobs (client never named).

Followed creators

Follow a profile once and every new post they publish lands in this inbox automatically, your archive to rewrite from. Checked about once a day; the first pull digs months back.

Approved media library

Upload brand-safe media once; attach it to drafts with one click. Generated quote cards and carousels land here too. A PDF publishes as a native LinkedIn document post (readers swipe through the pages), the format with the highest engagement on the platform right now.

Your voice

The rewriter follows this contract. The more real you make it, the more the drafts sound like you.

Posting schedule

Pick your slots once. "Approve + next slot" on any draft drops it into the next free one, so posting daily is two clicks. Times are your local time.

Publishing connection

Posts publish through your own RecruitersOS LinkedIn engine: the same connection the LinkedIn tool uses. Connect your LinkedIn account there once and the Poster rides it. Posting keeps a light footprint (a post or two a day), well inside the engine's safety pacing.

Backup publisher (optional alternative)

Official LinkedIn API partner, used only if you set AYRSHARE_API_KEY. Not required: the internal engine above is the default path.
